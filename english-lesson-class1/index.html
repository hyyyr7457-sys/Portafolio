🔶 Clase 1 – “Who We Are” (2 h)

🎯 Objetivo: describir ocupaciones y características personales

Warm‑up Grammar Vocabulary & Pronunciation Speaking Homework

1️⃣ Warm‑up 10 min

Juego “Guess the Profession” con imágenes (iconos) y pistas por audio.

Ejemplos a modelar

  • “I work in a court.” → “You’re a lawyer!”
  • “I study English three times a week.”
  • “I attend English classes on Tuesday, Thursday and Friday.”
  • “I am an eager student.”
